Features

A sparring partner
for pitches and sales calls

Most people rehearse a pitch in front of a mirror or a polite friend. In Hotseat, the investor or buyer across the table is paid to be skeptical.

Live voice, real interruptions

Speech to speech over OpenAI Realtime. The persona answers in its own voice about a second after you finish, lets you pause to think, and stops the moment you cut in.

Investors and clients

Six fictional personas, from a seed skeptic to a procurement lead, each with a brief, deal-breakers and a difficulty you can change. Paste your deck or key numbers and they'll ask about them.

A scorecard when you hang up

Six scored areas, what landed, what to fix and the toughest question they'd ask next, plus how you sounded: pace, filler words, share of the talking. See the change since your last call, and export it as Markdown.

Yours to run

One OpenAI key, which never reaches the browser. Your own password and rate limits, no database. Add a persona with a single JSON file.

How it works

From the brief
to the scorecard

A session takes as long as the real call would.

  1. Pick who's across the table

    An investor to pitch or a client to win.

  2. Read the brief

    What they care about and what will lose them.

  3. Talk or type

    Voice and chat share one conversation. Interrupt whenever you like.

  4. Get scored

    Hang up for a scorecard across six areas, then download it or go again.

Self-host

Self-host it
with one key

A single Next.js app with no database. Run it anywhere Node runs.

  • Needs only OPENAI_API_KEY and ADMIN_PASSWORD
  • Chat works with any OpenAI-compatible API; voice uses OpenAI
  • Password gate and per-client rate limits built in
